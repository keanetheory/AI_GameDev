## Analysis summary

The game is a strong tech spike for the sea and world, but it is not yet a playable Gate 1 slice. What exists and works: a first-person walker, a boat with one fixed speed and one steering axis, auto-docking and a hit-recovery trail, and a large greybox world. The world has the island, the lighthouse exterior, the harbour, the Saltgate quays on a 500 m sea corridor, cliffs, sea stacks and a storm lighting rig with ISLAND/OUTBOUND/RETURN/BEACON_RELIT phases. The dialogue data and a loader also exist. Almost everything that makes it a game is missing. There is no phase or state controller, and nothing in the level calls `DialogueLoader.trigger()`; the loader isn't even in `Level.tscn`, so no opening dialogue plays. There is no lighthouse interior or door, no Ted or Arthur in the scene, no boiler or coal job, no island gate or moored rowboat, no lantern room, no burner pickup or fit, no hidden deadline, no ship, no win or fail sequence, no restart, no HUD or subtitles, no audio, and no controller bindings. The crossing crew's `return_crossing.json` and `crossing_loader.gd` are not integrated, so the sea hazards are static rocks and cliffs with no buoys, debris or safe-route signal. The creator-decision backlog (fail reason line, Ted lock, deadline length, controller in prototype) also blocks parts of the build. The highest-value next step is a central phase and event controller. Everything else (interactions, outcomes, deadline) hangs off it. The next priorities are an interior greybox with the story interactions, then the burner and deadline loop, then the outcomes.
