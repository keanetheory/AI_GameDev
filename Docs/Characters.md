# Characters

## Ted - The Lighthouse Keeper

- **Role:** Keeper of the lighthouse and guardian of its beacon.
- **Age:** Late 50s.
- **Background:** Ted has lived and worked in the lighthouse for thirty years. The place, its machinery, and the sea around it are more familiar to him than ordinary company.
- **Appearance:** Burly and weather-beaten, with the heavy build of a man used to hauling, climbing, and repairing things by hand.
- **Personality:** Gruff, weary, and blunt. He has little patience for foolishness and initially holds the player in low regard, assuming they are another problem brought in by the sea.
- **Character direction:** His reserve should feel earned rather than cruel. Beneath the irritation is a tired sense of responsibility and a guarded concern for anyone caught in the lighthouse's failing protection.

## The Seamen

The two men are aboard a rowing boat that will soon crash near the lighthouse.

### Daniel - The Younger Seaman

- **Role:** Boatman and reluctant navigator.
- **Age:** 30s.
- **Background:** He was responsible for steering the boat and blames the lighthouse's weak, flickering beacon for the crash. The beacon was not completely dark, but it was giving too little light to guide them safely. Daniel was also sailing recklessly and put the boat at unnecessary risk despite the anger of the waves.
- **Personality:** More restrained than his companion, though defensive when blamed. He is frightened and angry, and clings to the beacon's failure because admitting his own recklessness would make him responsible for the crash.
- **Character direction:** Daniel is a useful but unreliable witness: he correctly identifies a lighthouse problem, but presents it as the whole cause of the crash to avoid facing his own poor judgment.

### Jeff - The Older Seaman

- **Role:** Experienced sailor and unwilling helper.
- **Age:** 60s.
- **Background:** A lifetime at sea has left him hard, suspicious, and thoroughly unimpressed by the lighthouse or the people around it.
- **Personality:** Coarse, sweary, and openly unhappy about helping the player. He complains constantly, but his instinct is still to keep people alive when things go bad.
- **Character direction:** He is relieved that the player is not Daniel - as Daniel was, in Jeff's view "a little reckless". That relief should briefly break through his hostility, revealing that he knows something about earlier arrivals or has been dreading being alone with the situation.
