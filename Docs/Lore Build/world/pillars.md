## Idea Backlog
- A grand city with a beautiful Gothic cathedral at its centre
- Fortresses a mile west (Kestrel) and east (Heron) of the lighthouse, lit and flagged but with no one visible on the walls
- The Marrow river, guarded by Fort Heron
- Greymantle, a giant mountain north of the city
- Documents and posters in the lighthouse praising the moon cycle and the Flighted
- A passenger ship heading for a dead harbour
