# Geography

## Physical Rules
Earth-like physics, geology, and weather. The only defined exception is the eclipse and its effect on tides and storms (see frontmatter).

## Landmasses & Oceans
- **Corvaine:** the mainland kingdom. Its coast faces the Gullwater.
- **Bracken Isle:** a small rocky island about 1–2 km offshore (a 10-minute steam-launch crossing), sitting on the approach to the Saltgate.
- **Gullwater:** the sea between Bracken Isle and the mainland, and the approach for ships bound for the Saltgate.

## Mountain Ranges (with cause)
- **Greymantle:** a giant mountain several miles north of Saint Calder. It is the source of the Marrow. Geological cause TBD.

## River Systems (source → mouth)
- **The Marrow:** runs from Greymantle south to the sea, reaching the coast about a mile east of the lighthouse, by Fort Heron. It is too small for the passenger ship, and Fort Heron guards its mouth.

## Climate Zones (with cause)
- Cold, wet, windswept maritime coast (proposed; latitude TBD).
- **Storm season:** driven by the eclipse season. With the moon covered, tides grow aggressive and storms follow.

## Winds & Currents
- Prevailing wind direction: TBD. It should match the lean of Bracken Isle's trees.
- Currents are mild outside eclipse season and violent within it, throwing ships against the rocks.

## Coastal Hazards
- Jagged rocks cover Bracken Isle and continue around it, many hidden just below the surface. This reef field is why the lighthouse exists.
- **The Saltgate:** jagged, with a clear channel in normal light. In storms, waves can throw a ship off course and onto the rocks.

## Natural Barriers & Corridors
- The reef field restricts approach to the Saltgate to a single channel.
- The Marrow is navigable only by small craft.
- A road runs from the Saltgate to Saint Calder, about a 20-minute walk (roughly 1.5 km).
