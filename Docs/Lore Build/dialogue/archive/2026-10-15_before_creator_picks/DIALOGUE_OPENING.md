# Opening Dialogue: Steps 1-3 (Gate 1 slice)

Status: **proposed**. Generated by the Opening Dialogue Crew (offline mode; 2 Dialogue-agent attempt(s), verdict PASS). The creator approves voice and lore before anything is recorded.

**Voice rules used:** Ted is gruff, snarky and old-school, calls city folk "new money toffs", and grunts and coughs. Arthur is wry, wiry and frantic under pressure. Daniel is light-hearted and anxious. The player, called Isaac, never speaks. Nothing mentions a timer, shows a marker or confirms a supernatural cause.

Lines reusing a canon sample line say so in their delivery note. **Pick one** means the game plays one option at random.

## Step 1: Feed the boiler

*Location:* Engine room (basement), then lantern room  
*Player does:* Shovel coal into the boiler, then find the beacon's burner is broken.

### On `GAME_STARTED`
*New game begins at the lighthouse entrance.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_01` (intro): "Hrmph. You're Isaac. Welcome to Bracken Isle. Try not to fall off it."  
  *delivery: gruff, dry; a cough after*
- **TED** `TED_S1_02` (setting): "No moon tonight. Something's sat on it again, and the sea hates it. Tides go savage, storms follow."  
  *delivery: flat, matter-of-fact; he has said this before*
- **TED** `TED_S1_03` (setting): "Across the water's the Saltgate. Dead harbour. The new money toffs in Saint Calder won't say why."  
  *delivery: bitter on 'new money toffs'*
- **TED** `TED_S1_04` (setting): "Out here, my light's all that keeps ships off the rocks. Which is why it had better work."  
  *delivery: gruff, a grunt on the last clause*
- **TED** `TED_S1_05` (objective): "Beacon runs on steam, steam runs on coal. Get down to the basement engine room and feed that boiler."  
  *delivery: barked order*
  *assist hint:* Go down to the basement engine room. Take the shovel, scoop coal from the heap and load the boiler.

### On `IDLE_STEP_1`
*Player has made no progress on step 1.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_N1` (nudge tier 1): "Well? Basement. Boiler. Coal. It's not complicated."  
  *delivery: impatient*
- **TED** `TED_S1_N2` (nudge tier 2): "What are you looking at, you a new money toff?? Get off your arse and sort out the light!"  
  *delivery: shouted; canon sample line*
- **TED** `TED_S1_N3` (nudge tier 3): "Stairs down, shovel by the coal heap, coal into the boiler. Slowly, if you must."  
  *delivery: weary, spelling it out*

### On `ENGINE_ROOM_ENTERED`
*Player enters the basement engine room.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_06` (reaction): "That big iron beast with the fire in it is the boiler. Shovel's by the heap."  
  *delivery: shouted down the stairwell*
  *staging:* Ted is above; his voice carries down the stairs.

### On `COAL_LOADED_FIRST`
*First shovelful lands in the firebox.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_07` (objective): "Good. Keep feeding her. Watch the gauge needle. It climbs when she's happy."  
  *delivery: grudging approval*
  *assist hint:* Keep shovelling until the steam gauge needle reaches the marked band.

### On `COAL_LOADED_HALF`
*Steam gauge reaches roughly half.*

- **Pick one:**
  - **TED** `TED_S1_08a` (reaction): "Keep going. She's thirsty."  
    *delivery: short, gruff*
  - **TED** `TED_S1_08b` (reaction): "More. A boiler's like a sailor. Never full."  
    *delivery: dry joke, a grunt after*

### On `BOILER_FED`
*Gauge reaches working pressure; dynamo whines, beacon flickers.*

- **Pick one:**
  - **TED** `TED_S1_09a` (objective): "There she goes! Hear the dynamo whine? Steam's up. Now get up to the lantern room."  
    *delivery: pleased, then barked*
    *assist hint:* Climb the stairs to the lantern room at the top of the lighthouse.
  - **TED** `TED_S1_09b` (objective): "Not bad for a new money toff. Steam's up. Lantern room, now, and mind the stairs."  
    *delivery: reluctant praise*
    *assist hint:* Climb the stairs to the lantern room at the top of the lighthouse.

### On `LAMP_ROOM_ENTERED`
*Player enters the lantern room.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_10` (objective): "See that? Flickering like a wet match. Steam's good, so the fault's in the lamp. Take a proper look."  
  *delivery: pointing, grim*
  *assist hint:* Walk up to the arc lamp and inspect the burner at its centre.
  *staging:* Ted is standing at the lamp.

### On `BURNER_DIAGNOSED`
*Player inspects the arc lamp and finds the burner broken.*

- **TED** `TED_S1_11` (reaction): "Cracked glass, rods burnt to stubs. That burner's finished. Hrmph. And I've no spare."  
  *delivery: quiet, then a grunt*
- **TED** `TED_S1_12` (reaction): "No burner, no light. No light, and the rocks do what rocks do."  
  *delivery: gallows humour, deadpan*

## Step 2: Let the sailors in

*Location:* Lantern-room window, path, island gate  
*Player does:* Look out of the window, go down the path and open the gate for Arthur and Daniel.

### On `SAILORS_MOORED`
*Rowboat appears moored at the dock (no crash).*

- **TED** `TED_S2_01` (objective): "Oi! Window! There's a rowboat tied up at the dock. Two men, soaked to the bone."  
  *delivery: sudden, urgent*
  *assist hint:* Walk to the lantern room window and look at the dock.
  *staging:* Ted is already at the window and sees the rowboat first.

### On `WINDOW_LOOKED`
*Player looks out of the window at the dock.*

- **TED** `TED_S2_02` (objective): "Fools, out in this. They'll want shelter, and the only way up is the gate. Go down and open it."  
  *delivery: gruff, then an order*
  *assist hint:* Leave the lighthouse, follow the path down to the iron gate and open it.

### On `IDLE_STEP_2`
*Player has made no progress on step 2.*

- **TED** `TED_S2_N1` (nudge tier 1): "The gate, Isaac. Those two won't dry themselves on the dock."  
  *delivery: impatient*
- **TED** `TED_S2_N2` (nudge tier 2): "Down the path to the iron gate. Open it. The sea isn't polite."  
  *delivery: sharper*
- **TED** `TED_S2_N3` (nudge tier 3): "Out the door, down the path, iron gate. Open it. Go."  
  *delivery: bellowed*

### On `GATE_APPROACHED`
*Player reaches the island gate.*

- **Pick one:**
  - **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S2_01a` (reaction): "Open the gate, would you? I've been wet since breakfast and I'm starting to take it personally!"  
    *delivery: shouted over the wind, wry*
  - **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S2_01b` (reaction): "Oi! Gate! Open up before the sea finishes what the storm started!"  
    *delivery: shouted, frantic*
- **DANIEL** `DANIEL_S2_01` (reaction): "Please! My hands have gone numb and I think my boots are full of fish!"  
  *delivery: high, anxious, a nervous laugh*

### On `GATE_OPENED`
*Player opens the island gate.*

- **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S2_02` (reaction): "Bless you. You'll be Isaac, then. Arthur. The shivering one's Daniel."  
  *delivery: friendly but wary*
- **DANIEL** `DANIEL_S2_02` (reaction): "Thank you, thank you! This was my first trip out. I might just stay on this flipping lighthouse!"  
  *delivery: light-hearted, anxious; canon sample line*
- **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S2_03` (reaction): "Don't ask what we were doing out there. Ask me when I'm dry."  
  *delivery: evasive, a snort*

### On `SAILORS_INSIDE`
*Arthur and Daniel reach the lighthouse hall.*

- **TED** `TED_S2_03` (reaction): "Arthur. Thought you'd have drowned by now."  
  *delivery: dry*
  *note:* Ted and Arthur's relationship is TBD in the lore; this keeps it neutral.
- **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S2_04` (reaction): "Lovely to see you too, Ted. Still charming the guests, I see."  
  *delivery: wry*
- **TED** `TED_S2_04` (reaction): "Warm's down in the engine room. Take the boy and dry out. Don't touch my boiler."  
  *delivery: gruff, to Daniel*

### On `DANIEL_IN_BASEMENT`
*Daniel reaches the basement and stays there.*

- **DANIEL** `DANIEL_S2_03` (reaction): "Oh, it's warm. It's lovely. Nobody mind me. I'm staying right here."  
  *delivery: relieved, slightly giddy*
  *staging:* Daniel stays in the basement for the rest of the slice.

## Step 3: Fetch the burner

*Location:* Dock, steam launch, outpost  
*Player does:* Board the launch with Arthur, cross the Gullwater and pick up the replacement burner.

### On `BURNER_BRIEFING`
*Player talks with Ted and Arthur in the hall.*

- **TED** `TED_S3_01` (setting): "Now listen. The burner's gone and I've no spare. There's one at the old outpost across the Gullwater."  
  *delivery: serious, gruff*
  *staging:* Ted talks with the player in the hall.
- **TED** `TED_S3_02` (setting): "Small thing, under a foot tall. Brass and iron, thick glass, carbon rods. Can't miss it."  
  *delivery: gruff, describing with his hands*
- **TED** `TED_S3_03` (objective): "Launch is at the dock. Arthur knows that water better than I do. Go with him and do as he says."  
  *delivery: an order, a cough*
  *assist hint:* Go to the dock and board the steam launch with Arthur.
- **Pick one:**
  - **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S3_01a` (reaction): "Cross the Gullwater? In this? Hrmph. Fine. That sky's turning nasty, and somebody has to do it."  
    *delivery: reluctant, then resolved*
  - **ARTHUR** `ARTHUR_S3_01b` (reaction): "Back out there? I've only just got dry! ...Aye, all right. Better the light's mended than we're sorry."  
    *delivery: groaning, then resolved*
