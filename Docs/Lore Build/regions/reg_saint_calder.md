# Saint Calder

> **Prototype scope:** visible in the distance only. Playable in a future expansion.

## Geography & Climate (and why)
- The capital-like city of Corvaine, inland behind the Saltgate and linked to it by a road (about a 20-minute walk).
- Greymantle rises several miles to the north and feeds the Marrow.

## Key Locations
- **The cathedral:** a grand, beautiful 1800s Gothic cathedral at the city's centre.
- **The city:** mid-1800s architecture, with windows lit by steam-driven electric light.

## Current Tensions
- Unfriendly or closed off. It abandoned its own harbour.
- Its royal standard (amber and gold) hangs neglected and tattered on the coast.
