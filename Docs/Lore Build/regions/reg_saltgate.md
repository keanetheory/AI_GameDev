# The Saltgate

## Geography & Climate (and why)
- The harbour of Saint Calder, on the coast directly across the Gullwater from Bracken Isle.
- Jagged, with a clear channel in normal light. In storms, waves can throw a ship off course and onto the rocks.
- **During eclipses:** the port's light is almost completely gone, so the lighthouse is the only reliable guide.
