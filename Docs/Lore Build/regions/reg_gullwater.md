# The Gullwater

## Geography & Climate (and why)
- The sea between Bracken Isle and the Saltgate. The crossing takes about 10 minutes by steam launch (roughly 1–2 km).
- A submerged reef rings Bracken Isle.
- Tides are aggressive in eclipse season.

## The Crossing (gameplay)
- **Outbound:** mild currents sway the boat a little. Calm and uneasy.
- **Return:** current, wind, and rain build into a loud storm. Jeff guides the player through it.
- **Vessel:** a small steam launch with a mini steam engine.
- **Visible landmarks:**
  - Fort Kestrel to the west and Fort Heron to the east, lit and flagged with no one visible
  - The Saltgate ahead
  - The lighthouse behind
  - The passenger ship on the horizon

## Current Tensions
- The passenger ship is approaching the reef.
- Smugglers run the waters, slipping past the fortresses.
