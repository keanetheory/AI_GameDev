# The Eclipse

> **L4 incomplete.** Effects on people are the core open question.

## How It Works (input → effect)
- **Input:** each season, a dark spherical object hovers over the moon and blocks it out. Iconography hints that it is a ship.
- **Effects:**
  - The moon is hidden, and the port's light is almost completely gone.
  - Tides become more aggressive.
  - Storm season begins.
  - People get "messed up" by the moon cycles. **Mechanics TBD.** This is the real horror of the game.
