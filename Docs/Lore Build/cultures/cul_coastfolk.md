# Coastfolk

## Surface Identity (L3)

### Architecture
Stone and mortar built to withstand the weather, like the lighthouse: battered but robust.

### Everyday Clothing
Oilskins, hefty wool jumpers against the cold, and heavy black, oily boots. Individuals are distinguished by the colour of their oilskins and jumpers:
- Ted wears navy.
- Jeff wears green.
- Daniel wears red.

### Armor & Weapons
None (civilians).

### Symbols & Heraldry
They live under Saint Calder's amber-and-gold standard, which hangs neglected on the coast.

### Silhouette Description
Bulky and weather-hunched, with layered oilskin coats over thick jumpers and heavy boots.

## Internal Variation
Coastfolk are not one uniform people. Locals fall into several factions:
- [[fac_farmers]]: care nothing for politics
- [[fac_smugglers]]: slip in and out of Saint Calder past the fortresses
- [[fac_resistance]]: believe the Flighted are evil
- [[fac_political_class]]: revere the Flighted but can't communicate with them
- **Keepers and sailors:** Ted, Jeff, and Daniel

They prey on and are preyed on by [[fac_city_elite]].
