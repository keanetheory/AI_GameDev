# The Flighted

> **Prototype scope:** heard about only briefly. They are "something," but nobody knows what. They are bound up with the eclipse: two sides of the same central mystery. Direct contact comes in a later expansion.

## Goal
Unknown.

## Origin (link events)
Unknown. Possibly tied to the dark sphere over the moon ([[sys_eclipse]]).

## Beliefs & Internal Divisions
How others see them:
- [[fac_political_class]] reveres them.
- [[fac_resistance]] believes they are evil.
- [[fac_farmers]] don't care.

## Propaganda (in-world documents & posters)
- **Imagery:** the moon and waves. A dark spherical object, hinting at a ship, eclipsing the moon.
- **Slogans:**
  - "They bring the storms."
  - "They change the seasons."
- **Tone:** reverent, but also somewhat fearful.
- **Colours:** always black, silver (the moon), and navy.
- **Print quality:** crude but clear.
