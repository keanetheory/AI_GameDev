# Cliffside: concept art comparison

Compared 2026-10-20 by the Cliffside Creator Agent against in-engine captures from the real level (Godot render, not a mock-up). Concept: `ConceptArt/channel_scenes/position of both saltgate and the cliff terrain.png`, `ConceptArt/cliff_terrain/Mossy slate coastal cliff module-1.png`.

| Aspect | Concept art | Build now | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cliff face | Tall slate columns, grey with gold lichen | Same module meshes, same painted texture | Match |
| Cliff top | Yellow-olive moss and grass, broken rock | Same texture on the cliff top, olive-yellow grass plateau behind | Match |
| Coastline shape | Headlands and coves, jagged outcrops | One long straight wall (ten modules end to end) | **Difference, accepted for now**: needs new modules to change |
| Base of the cliff | Rubble and scree at the foot, surf and spray | Bare cliff meeting a flat sea | **Difference**: no rubble, no surf. Candidate for the Wave agent (spray) and a small prop pass |
| Trees | Tall, slender, dark conifers in varied heights, dense | Short, wide low-poly cones, now darker and leaning, kept 10 m back from the lip | Closer than before; **still stylised**; a slimmer mesh would match better |
| Walkway | A track along the cliff with timber piers at the quay | Rock ledge of the cliff's own material, timber boardwalks at the quay | Match in spirit; timber/rock boundary is the creator's decision (CLF-08) |
| Quay | Stone and timber piers hard against the cliff foot | Quay set against the cliff with the joint contract still to be agreed | Pending the Saltgate agent |
| Lighting | Dark storm, cold grey-teal, warm window glow | Matches in OUTBOUND; on the RETURN fog hides the face beyond about 150 m | Lighting agent's setting |

Not checked: how the cliff reads from the boat in daylight-like conditions (there are none in the game) and any comparison at the same camera angles as the concept, which is a hand-drawn view.
