# Lighting the Storm: playtest sheet (Gate 1 slice)

Thank you for playing. The slice is short (about 10 to 15 minutes). Please play it once without help, then answer the questions at the bottom. There is no wrong answer: where you got stuck is exactly what we need to know.

## 1. Install and run (Windows)

- You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a graphics card or chip with Vulkan support, 4 GB of RAM (8 GB better) and about 900 MB of free disk space to unpack.
- Unzip `LightingTheStorm_windows.zip` anywhere (right-click, **Extract All**). Keep `LightingTheStorm.exe` and `LightingTheStorm.pck` together in the same folder.
- Double-click `LightingTheStorm.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the game is not signed yet).
- On a laptop with two graphics chips, the game normally picks the faster one. If it runs badly, tell us your laptop model.
- Headphones help: the characters' voices come from where they stand.

## 2. Controls

Mouse to look, **W A S D** to walk, **Shift** to run, **Space** to jump, **E** to use or pick up, **Q** to step off the boat, **J** journal, **C** shows or hides the controls list, **P** pauses. At the helm, **A** and **D** steer (the boat keeps its own speed). On the title screen, **Left / Right** chooses Easy, Medium or Hard. After the ending, **Enter** plays again.

## 3. What to try

Play it your way first. Afterwards, if you have time, try one or two of these:

- Take your time at each step: wander, read things, go back into rooms. Does anyone nag you, and is it helpful or annoying?
- Go down to the basement a few times after the sailors arrive. Does Daniel have something to say?
- Try to break it: shut the gate again while the sailors walk through, get in the boat before Arthur, walk off the jetty into the sea, carry things to odd places.
- On the way back with the burner, hit a rock or two on purpose. Can you still make it?
- Let the ship be lost once (dawdle on the way back), then press Enter and play again.
- Play once on Hard.

## 4. Questions (a line or two each is plenty)

1. How long did it take you, start to finish? Which difficulty?
2. Did you win or lose? Did you understand why?
3. Where were you stuck, or unsure what to do next? What did you try?
4. Was there anything you did not understand (a line, a place, what someone wanted)?
5. Did any line of speech get cut off, talk over another, or come at the wrong moment?
6. Did anything look broken: black areas, a character in a wall, a missing object, falling through the floor?
7. Did it ever stutter or feel slow? Where were you? (On a laptop: plugged in or on battery?)
8. Steering the boat: too easy, fair, or too hard?
9. The ending: after the light comes on, how did the wait for the ship feel?
10. What was the best moment, and the worst?

## 5. Sending it back

- Screenshots: press **Windows + Shift + S** (or **PrtScn**) and paste into your message. A phone photo of the screen is fine too.
- Say roughly where you were and what you had just done ("on the tower stairs, carrying the burner").
- Tell us your Windows version and graphics (Start, type `dxdiag`, the **Display** tab shows the card name).
- Send the answers and pictures to the creator by email or chat. If the game crashes, a screenshot of any message and what you were doing is enough.
