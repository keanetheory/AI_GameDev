## In one paragraph

Stepping off the boat at the Saltgate is now smooth and tested: 8 of 8 consecutive runs of the new `tools/test_disembark.gd` pass, and all 11 regression tests pass. Three things were wrong and are fixed. Stepping ashore was a teleport. The landing put you on top of a walled stone block with a 1.3 m drop on every side. The launch tied up 5.7 m off the quay. The timber link ramp to the cliff walkway had two of the quay's old piles standing up through it, and is moved. The quay meshes audit clean, and the quay's texture is now 2048 (file 30.9 to 14.7 MB). Audio anchors and the concept comparison are done. Still not done: the port does not yet "grow out of" the cliff, the Saint Calder glow cannot be seen from the quay, and SLT-04 and SLT-05 wait on you.
