# Style Guide: Lighting the Storm

Generated by `build.py` from `style_guide.json`. Every rule is pulled from the Lore Build, the final GDD (Executive Summary, AI Agent Strategy) or the project's `canon.py`; the source of each is cited.

**Scope:** Spoken and subtitled dialogue for Ted, Arthur and Daniel (the content class the Dialogue agent produces). The same rule pack is the base for journal text and poster copy.

**Record layout:** `SPEAKER [EVENT_ID]: "line text"`, at most 22 words of text per line.

## Voices

- **TED**: Lighthouse master and the player's boss. Gruff, snarky, old-school. Grunts and coughs. Calls city folk 'new money toffs'. Never on the boat.
- **ARTHUR**: Older sailor, wiry, wry, frantic under pressure. Friendly but wary. Short practical directions shouted over wind. The only companion on the crossings.
- **DANIEL**: Younger sailor, light-hearted and anxious, strong and heavy but immature. Stays in the basement by the boiler. Never on the boat.
