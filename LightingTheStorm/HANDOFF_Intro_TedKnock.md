## 3. How to run and test

```bash
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/test_intro_sequence.gd
```

The opening: the player stands outside the shut door and sees "[E] Knock". E knocks (the knock sounds, nothing else opens); 1.3 s later GAME_STARTED fires and Ted greets them (TED_S1_01, the one Ted line spoken with the player outside); 6.5 s after that Ted opens the door as the greeting ends on "In."; the door is then an ordinary door. When the player steps inside (LIGHTHOUSE_ENTERED) Ted's coal order (TED_S1_03) plays and Ted walks the 66 m up the tower to the lamp room (about 53 s at 1.15 m/s), where he waits beside the arc lamp. Levels with no front door (a test scene) start the game at once as before.

"Inside the building" is the house footprint (any floor, including the basement) or the tower (above y 9 within 3.4 m of its axis). A Ted line triggered while the player is outside is held (not spoken, not marked played) and speaks as soon as they come in; a Ted nudge (IDLE_*) is dropped instead. Arthur's, Daniel's and everyone else's lines are not affected (Daniel's keep their own basement rule).
