# HANDOFF - T14: placeholder audio (wind, rain, machinery, bells, foghorn, impact), mixed by phase

## 1. Task and goal

Placeholder audio buses and players for wind, rain, boiler and dynamo, buoy bells, the foghorn and the ship's impact, mixed by phase, with Arthur's calls kept audible; source and licence recorded per file. Serves R18 and the sound half of R11 (storm channel follows elapsed deadline time) and of the fail sequence (A30).

## 2. Files changed

New:
- `Assets/Audio/placeholder/*.wav` (7 files, about 2 MB) and `SOURCES.md` (source and licence per file). `tools/make_placeholder_audio.py` synthesises them (numpy, fixed seed, CC0); Godot's `.import` files are committed with them.
- `data/world/audio_spec.json`: every level, the per-phase mix, duck, indoor muffle, bell positions and the fail timings.
- `scripts/core/audio_mixer.gd`: creates the Ambience and SFX buses, the players, and mixes them.
- `tools/test_audio_mixer.gd`.

Edited: `scripts/core/game_state.gd` (creates the mixer with the level, `audio()` accessor), `readme.md` (test list).

Not touched: the Voice bus (jaw_lipsync.gd), Level.tscn, the lighthouse interior, any dialogue audio.

## 3. How to run and test

```bash
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/test_audio_mixer.gd
godot --headless --path . --import      # after adding or changing any .wav
python tools/make_placeholder_audio.py  # regenerate the placeholder sounds
```

How the mix works: `GameState` makes an `AudioMixer` inside the level. The phase picks each layer's dB from `audio_spec.json`; on the return, wind, rain and engine follow `GameState.deadline_progress()` (a frozen clock keeps the storm where it was). Inside the house footprint (`InteriorCulling.inside`) a low-pass muffles the ambience. While anyone speaks (`Dialogue.line_started` to `line_finished`) the ambience ducks by 6 dB. The engine plays only while the boat is under way. Bells are three 3D sources by the return route, ringing at random.

Fail sequence (A30): at `SHIP_FAIL` the foghorn sounds from the ship three times (5 s apart, all before the ship strikes at about 13.6 s), the impact plays on the strike, 5 s later both buses fade to silence over 2 s, and after 1.5 s of silence the mixer emits `fail_audio_finished` (T08 shows the Failed screen on it). A restart returns the buses to full level.

## 4. Observed behaviour

`test_audio_mixer.gd`: OK, 0 failed (24 checks): sounds load and loop; the boiler is cold at arrival and comes up after `BOILER_FED`; no engine unless under way; the return is louder than the island and follows the deadline clock (-12, -7, -2.2 dB for wind at progress 0, 0.5, 1); the indoor muffle, the duck and its release; the foghorn blasts at 0.1, 5.1 and 10.1 s, before the strike at 13.6 s; `fail_audio_finished` fires once, 22.0 s after the deadline; a restart restores the buses. Earlier ship, state, lamp-fit and route tests still pass with the mixer in the level.

- Buoy bells ring at three guessed positions, not at real buoys (T10).
