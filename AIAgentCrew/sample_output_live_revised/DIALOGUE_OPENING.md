# Opening Dialogue: Steps 1-3 (Gate 1 slice)

Status: **proposed**. Generated by the Opening Dialogue Crew (llm mode; 3 Dialogue-agent attempt(s), verdict PASS). The creator approves voice and lore before anything is recorded.

**Voice rules used:** Ted is gruff, snarky and old-school, calls city folk "new money toffs", and grunts and coughs. Jeff is wry, wiry and frantic under pressure. Daniel is light-hearted and anxious. The player, called the new guy, never speaks. Nothing mentions a timer, shows a marker or confirms a supernatural cause.

Lines reusing a canon sample line say so in their delivery note. **Pick one** means the game plays one option at random.
