# HANDOFF - WORLD SPECS (boat, player movement, lighting)

## 1. Task and goal
Specify the steam launch (dock <-> Saltgate port, fixed speed, A/D steering), smooth on-foot movement, and the lighting rig (eclipsed moon, lightning, lighthouse lights, distant Saint Calder) for the first traversable Godot level.

## 2. Files changed
- `boat_spec.json`, `player_movement_spec.json`, `lighting_spec.json`
- `handoffs/01_boat.json` .. `04_review.json`

## 3. How to run and test
- Regenerate: `python main_world.py --offline`
- Godot: the specs are data. Read them from the boat controller, `player.gd` and a lighting rig script (not written yet).

## 4. Observed behaviour
Boat, Player Movement and Lighting specs for the first traversable Godot level. All deterministic checks pass. Steering feel, walking feel, flash safety and hazard readability need creator playtesting. Tide and wave behaviour is deliberately not specified yet.

- [PASS] 1. The light must return: Lighting phases end at BEACON_RELIT; RETURN ends at DOCK_LIGHTHOUSE.
- [PASS] 2. The storm sets the clock: Lightning, fog and visibility are driven by phase and route position; no timers in any spec.
- [PASS] 3. Your hands, your eyes: First-person walking and A/D steering only; beacon and Saint Calder are scenery with is_navigation_marker false.
- [PASS] 4. Dread you can read: RETURN hazard_visibility_m 70 with lightning that reveals hazards; 7 glitch checks with tests.
- [PASS] One fixed speed, A/D steering: speed_mps 3.0; throttle_actions empty; W and S do nothing.
- [PASS] No waves or tides: waves_enabled False; tide_hook reserved for the Tide / Wave agent.
- [PASS] No visible timer or markers: All is_navigation_marker flags false; no timer wording.
- [PASS] Moon absent or eclipsed: moon.state 'eclipsed'.
- [PASS] Hazards readable: legibility_checks cover hazards, lightning, beacon and Saint Calder.
